About Me

I received my B.Eng. degree in Automation in 2019 and Ph.D. degree in Cyberspace Security in 2024 both from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), under the supervision of Prof. Yan Chen. From July 2024 to October 2026, I worked as a postdoctoral researcher at USTC. I am currently an Assistant Professor in the Department of Informatics and Communication Engineering at Xiamen University.
